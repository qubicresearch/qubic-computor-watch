What we can and can’t say: the collection-hub-to-exchange half of this chain (steps 1-3) is fully verified, current, and repeats reliably. A naive scan of “recent transactions” during the still-open current epoch found no sweeps at all — because the real payout happens on a delay: an epoch’s revenue is computed after it closes and paid out roughly two days into the next epoch, in one exact lump sum, not continuously. Once we looked at the right moment instead of the wrong window, we found it immediately: all 5 long-tenured computors checked received a direct payout from the network’s own protocol address, then forwarded the exact same amount, in full, to a known MinerLab/JetSki hub within about 1,377 ticks (minutes) — a perfect amount match every single time (e.g. 519,035,552 QU in, 519,035,552 QU out). This closes the loop completely: seed in from a hub, network pays real revenue directly to the computor once per epoch, 100% of that revenue is swept straight back out to a hub within minutes, repeat.

The mechanism, in brief

Every candidate identity is auto-funded by pool software (not manually by an operator) the moment it's first used — reproduced directly on independent hardware, four separate times, with zero operator involvement. Each real work submission posts a 1,000,000 QU bond that the network independently re-verifies and only refunds if the recomputed solution matches exactly. Winning a seat isn't a flat contest across all 676: the top 451 ranked identities are copied forward automatically each epoch, while the remaining 225 seats are recomputed after every single valid submission network-wide — a new identity only has to beat the current worst of that 225, not the whole network.
