JetSki — real payout wallets

No epoch-230 computor pays a JetSki-branded wallet directly. JETSKIYUBUPF, JETSKIQRDEIV, and JETSKIMZKTSL only appear downstream of the shared clearinghouse (see below) — JetSki is the payout/dispatch stage of this operation, not a separate collection stage. This epoch's data doesn't support treating MinerLab and JetSki as two parallel, independent pools.

Tied together: one real chain, both brands

Every MinerLab-branded hub above (ZTQM, MLAB*) and every unbranded hub (YNRE, TMNN, TKOG) has been directly traced sending its accumulated balance to the same address: LKBOPOUKGSYTODWVEPXHUXDTRSOCDOXXXIEAGBJXAGJRMGXRXMCHDNCHWRLK (the clearinghouse). LKBOP then fans that combined balance out to the three JETSKI-branded payout wallets, plus GXITF (which forwards on to BBMMA, which forwards to the confirmed MEXC exchange wallet VUEY). One real trace, verified 2026-09-02: TMNN sent 13,133,333,333 QU to LKBOP at tick 77,704,631; LKBOP fanned 26,952,629,830 QU of its combined balance out to JETSKIYUBUPF less than three hours later. Full trace with all hops: main investigation page.

Method & limits

  • Computor set: rpc.qubic.org/v1/epochs/230/computors (676, official).
  • Family/hub resolution: KNOWN dict carried forward, then largest real outgoing transfer ≥100,000,000 QU traced to a confirmed hub, then (for the remainder) cross-checked directly against the protocol's own per-computor revenue record.
  • MinerLab vs JetSki split is based on brand naming already documented in our KNOWN dict (MLAB*/ZTQM = MinerLab-labeled; JETSKI* = JetSki-labeled) plus this epoch's real destination-hub trace. Hubs without an exclusive brand attribution are shown separately, not guessed into one side.
  • This is flow + revenue-record analysis, not legal ownership.
  • Every address is checkable at explorer.qubic.org, or below.
