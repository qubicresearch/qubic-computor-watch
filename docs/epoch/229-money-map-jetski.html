Vote breakdown — ZTQM vs TMNN, epoch 229's 3 real proposals

3 real governance proposals were live in epoch 229: GQMPROP GeneralOptions (proposal tick 77994817, proposer qli) and two CCF Transfer proposals (78369421 and 78369500, both proposer jetski). Real vote-cast transactions decoded from the CCF/GQMPROP contracts' own transfer history.

Proposal 77994817 (GQMPROP GeneralOptions)
ZTQM: 51/51 voted. TMNN: 24/25 voted (one seat, RLGYBGHHIRLW…, has zero votes on this specific proposal — it did vote on the other two).
Timing: ZTQM's full 51 + 9 of TMNN's 25 voted together within ~2 seconds (ticks 78942712–78944711). The remaining 15 TMNN computors voted separately, ~203,000 ticks later (ticks 79146182–79146202, roughly 2.4 days on).
Proposal 78369421 (CCF Transfer, proposer jetski)
ZTQM: 50/51 voted. TMNN: 25/25 voted.
Timing: same two-wave pattern — ZTQM's block + a small TMNN slice together first (ticks 78835939–78944795), remainder of TMNN following later (ticks 79146265–79146279).
Proposal 78369500 (CCF Transfer, proposer jetski)
ZTQM: 50/51 voted. TMNN: 25/25 voted.
Timing: same pattern again — ZTQM's block + a small TMNN slice together first (ticks 78835877–78944848), remainder of TMNN following later (ticks 79146340–79146357).

On all 3 proposals, the small TMNN slice that voted alongside ZTQM was verified against the classification cache — each of those computors' own largest real epoch-229 payout goes directly to the TMNN wallet, same as the rest of TMNN. They're correctly classified; this is a real timing split in how the operator cast votes, not a data error.
