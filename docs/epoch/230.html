Method & limits

  • Computor set for epoch 230: rpc.qubic.org/v1/epochs/230/computors (676 identities, official).
  • Family resolution, in order: (1) KNOWN dict carried forward from prior epochs' own verification, (2) largest real outgoing transfer ≥100,000,000 QU this epoch traced to an already-confirmed hub (rpc.qubic.org/query/v1/getEventLogs), (3) for the remainder, cross-checked directly against analytics.qubic.li/api/stats/computor-revenue?epoch=230 — the protocol's own per-computor revenue-and-owner record, not a money-flow inference.
  • New hubs TCRX/FSDR/TOUP/VOYI were added to the KNOWN dict only after confirming they forward directly to already-established qli wallets (QLIFODMBOSRWEAPSTZWF, QLIWZKBVQEKQADKZLPZL, QLIXZOGTLJVHWBZLJODF, QLINAMIOESDCZCCDTMII, QLIKKBOCYDIMXBAOKUTE) — a direct transaction-graph link, not a shared-destination guess.
  • "Real revenue traced to this hub" sums the protocol's own per-computor revenue figure (analytics.qubic.li), not the traced transfer amount itself — the two are usually within a few percent of each other but not identical, since a computor's real payout amount and its forwarded amount can differ by small on-chain fees/timing.
  • This is flow + revenue-record analysis. It does not establish legal ownership of any computor, only where its epoch-230 revenue is demonstrably traceable.
  • Every address above is independently checkable at explorer.qubic.org, or with the lookup tool below.
